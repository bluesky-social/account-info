What is account.info?

A service for fetching AT Protocol account information by handle or DID.

Endpoints

  • /{handle-or-DID} account identity, PDS, and profile records
  • /avatar/{handle-or-DID} original profile avatar (JPEG or PNG)

Examples

Look up a handle:

curl https://account.info/calabro.io

Or use a DID:

curl https://account.info/did:plc:4uz2445cjiw7w4nobfgnu35f

Download an avatar:

curl -o avatar.jpg https://account.info/avatar/calabro.io

Add ?all=true to include every supported profile record found for the account.

account.info is open source and built on the AT Protocol.
