What is account.info?

A service for fetching AT Protocol account information by handle or DID.

{{end}}

Endpoints

  • /{handle-or-DID} public profile page or complete JSON account details
  • /avatar/{handle-or-DID} original profile avatar (JPEG, PNG, or WebP)

Examples

Look up a handle:

curl https://account.info/bsky.app

Or use a DID:

curl https://account.info/did:plc:z72i7hdynmk6r22z27h6tvur

Download an avatar:

curl https://account.info/avatar/bsky.app

Or via the Accept header:

curl -L -H 'Accept: image/*' https://account.info/bsky.app

account.info is open source and built on the AT Protocol.
